SC INTEGRATOR BULK DESIGN GUIDANCE

Use only the measured, functionally valid D0/AL observations and the current
runtime parameter bounds. The sign of a response is topology-dependent; do
not assume that every integrator is inverting. A failed simulator or missing
measurement is not a zero-valued metric. Propose exactly one parameter vector
in the generic JSON-only x_seed schema. Do not modify clocks, bias, models,
topology, target specifications, or physical budget.
